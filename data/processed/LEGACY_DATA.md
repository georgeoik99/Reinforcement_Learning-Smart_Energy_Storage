# Legacy one-year data

`energy_hourly_2025.csv` is retained unchanged for historical reference only.
It belongs to the obsolete Stage-3/Stage-4 experiment (~47 kWp PV, 70/15/15 split).
It is **not** the 2025 test slice used by Stage 4.5.

The active dataset is `energy_hourly_2023_2025.csv`, regenerated by
`python -m src.data.generate_synthetic_energy_data`. Use 2023 for training,
2024 for validation and 2025 for test. Legacy results/models/figures are in
`outputs/archive/pre_stage4_5/`; the pre-refinement repository snapshot and
SHA-256 inventory are archived alongside them.
